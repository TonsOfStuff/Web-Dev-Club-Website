About Us

We're a club at New Trier that's dedicated to creating websites, learning about creating code, and working together to understand the web better. The internet has become such a commonplace in our society, yet many people don't understand the cogs behind the machine. Here at this club, our goal is help people understand the place that they are surfing by providing real experience with code. No prior programming experience! Make anything that you want and we'll be there to support you!
